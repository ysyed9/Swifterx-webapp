Privacy Policy – SwifterX

Effective Date: April 4, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 4, 2026

SwifterX (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the SwifterX mobile application (the “App”) for iOS and other platforms. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information. Please read it together with Apple’s standard privacy disclosures for your device.

App Store privacy information. The data types and purposes you declare in App Store Connect (App Privacy labels) for SwifterX must accurately reflect the collection and use practices described in this policy. Keep labels and this policy in sync when your practices change.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account: name, email address, and password. Passwords are processed using industry-standard one-way hashing; we do not store your password in plain text.
  • Profile (optional): profile photo (e.g. from your photo library or camera, if you choose), bio
  • Service providers: services offered, pricing, availability, service area
  • Bookings: service address, special instructions, scheduled date and time
  • Communication: in-app messages and reviews you submit

Information Collected Automatically

Location

  • Precise location: collected only when you grant permission and only as needed for active jobs (for example, live tracking and coordination between customer and provider). You can withdraw location permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services on your iPhone; some features may not work without it.
  • Approximate location: may be used to show nearby providers or relevant results when you use search or discovery features, consistent with your permission settings.

We do not use your location to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Device, usage, and identifiers

  • IP address, device type, operating system version, and general app diagnostics
  • Product interaction and usage events (for example, screens or features used, session duration) collected through our analytics tools
  • Push notification token (via Apple Push Notification service and our notification infrastructure) so we can send job updates and alerts you opt into

Diagnostics and performance

  • Crash logs, error reports, and performance data. These may include device model, OS version, and technical information to help us fix bugs. They are not used to sell your data or for cross-app advertising tracking.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe’s collection and use of payment information is governed by its privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. We do not store your full payment card number on our servers.

2. Tracking and Advertising

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the App to track you across third-party apps or websites for advertising purposes as described in Apple’s developer documentation on tracking. We do not request the App Tracking Transparency prompt for cross-app advertising tracking on behalf of SwifterX.

Analytics and crash reporting are used to operate and improve the App, not to sell data or to enable cross-app behavioral advertising by SwifterX.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal information to:

  • Create and secure your account and authenticate you
  • Match customers with service providers and display relevant listings
  • Process bookings, payments, and payouts (through Stripe)
  • Provide in-app messaging, reviews, and customer support
  • Provide real-time job status and location features during active jobs, when you have allowed them
  • Send push notifications and in-app notices about jobs and your account (you can manage notifications in iOS Settings and in the App where available)
  • Monitor reliability, diagnose crashes, and improve performance and features
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our terms

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share information with:

  • Other users, as needed to provide the service (for example, sharing limited booking details between a customer and a provider)
  • Stripe — payment processing: stripe.com/privacy
  • Google LLC (Firebase) — we use Firebase components that may process data on our behalf, including:
    • Firebase Authentication — sign-in and account security
    • Cloud Firestore / Realtime Database (as applicable) — storing account and app data
    • Firebase Analytics — product analytics
    • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting
    See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Professional advisers, acquirers, or regulators where permitted by law
  • Law enforcement or government entities when required by valid legal process

We require service providers who process personal data for us to use appropriate safeguards and process data only as instructed.

5. Location Data (Summary)

  • Live, precise location is used only during active jobs and with permission, for tracking and coordination.
  • We do not retain precise live location indefinitely after the job is completed beyond what is needed for support, safety, or legal record-keeping.
  • Approximate location may be used for discovery features as described in Section 1.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account, subject to legal holds
  • Booking and transaction records: may be retained for up to seven (7) years where required for tax, accounting, or legal compliance
  • Analytics and diagnostics: retained for limited periods according to Firebase/Google configuration and our operational needs

You may request deletion of your account or personal data as described in Section 7. Some information may be retained where the law requires.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access or receive a copy of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account or certain data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (for example, turning off location or notifications in Settings)

California (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law. California residents may request access or deletion by emailing us.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland: If you are in these regions, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law (including complaints to a supervisory authority). The contact email below is the primary point of contact for privacy requests.

To exercise your rights, contact: swifterxtech@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law (often within 30 days for many requests).

8. International Transfers

We may process and store information in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Those countries may have different data protection laws than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us at swifterxtech@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (such as TLS)
  • Secure cloud infrastructure and access controls
  • Hashed passwords and authentication safeguards

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Services

The App relies on third-party services whose privacy practices are described in their own policies, including Stripe and Google Firebase. We encourage you to read those policies. Their use of information is governed by their terms; we select providers we believe meet industry standards.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the App (and on this webpage where applicable) and update the “Last Updated” date above. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice as appropriate (for example, an in-app notice). Continued use of the App after the effective date of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, except where your consent is required by law.
