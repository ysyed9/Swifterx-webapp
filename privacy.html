Privacy Policy – SwifterX

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

SwifterX (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the SwifterX mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account: name, email, password (encrypted)
  • Profile (optional): photo, bio
  • Service Providers: services, pricing, availability, service area
  • Bookings: service address, instructions, date/time
  • Communication: messages and reviews

Information Collected Automatically

Location Data

  • Precise location (only during active jobs, with permission)
  • Approximate location (to show nearby providers)

Device & Usage Data

  • IP address
  • Device type, OS
  • App usage (pages visited, time spent)

Diagnostics

  • Crash logs and performance data

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store full card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Match customers with service providers
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Provide real-time job tracking
  • Enable in-app messaging
  • Send notifications (job updates, alerts)
  • Improve app performance and features
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data with:

  • Service Providers / Customers (to fulfill bookings)
  • Stripe (payments) — stripe.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting) — Firebase Privacy
  • Legal Authorities (if required by law)

All third parties are required to protect your data.

4. Location Data

  • Live location is shared only during active jobs
  • Used for tracking and coordination
  • Not stored permanently after job completion

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: until you delete your account
  • Booking & transaction data: up to 7 years (legal compliance)
  • Analytics data: limited retention (e.g., Firebase defaults)

You may request deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct your data
  • Delete your account
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request a copy of your data

To request this, contact: swifterxtech@gmail.com

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS)
  • Secure data storage
  • Encrypted passwords

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Google Firebase (analytics, infrastructure)

We are not responsible for their practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted in the App. Continued use means you accept the changes.
